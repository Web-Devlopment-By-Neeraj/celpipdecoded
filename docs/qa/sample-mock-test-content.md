## Listening

Part title: Library hours
part_type: listening_daily_conversation

Conversation:

Maya: The main library closes early on Friday. Do you still want to meet there?
Leo: Yes. Friday it closes at 4. We can meet at 2 and still have time.
Maya: Should we sit on the second floor?
Leo: The second floor is quiet, but the study rooms are on the first floor. Let's book a study room.

Audio link, copy exactly if you want the player to have a clip. Leaving it blank is allowed. The upload will warn and still save:

https://www.soundhelix.com/examples/mp3/SoundHelix-Song-1.mp3

Question 1
Prompt: What time does the library close on Friday?
A: At noon
B: At 2
C: At 4
D: At 6
Correct: C
Explanation: Leo says Friday it closes at 4.

Question 2
Prompt: Where do they decide to work?
A: On the second floor
B: In a study room on the first floor
C: In the cafe
D: At home
Correct: B
Explanation: Leo says the study rooms are on the first floor and suggests booking one.

## Reading, message

Part title: Community garden email
part_type: reading_correspondence

Message:

Hello neighbours,

The community garden work day is Saturday at 9 am. Please bring gloves. We will plant tomatoes on the west bed and herbs on the east bed. Coffee will be ready at the shed from 8:30.

Thanks,
Nora

Question 1
Prompt: When is the work day?
A: Friday at 4
B: Saturday at 9 am
C: Sunday at noon
D: Saturday at 8:30 only
Correct: B
Explanation: Nora says the work day is Saturday at 9 am. 8:30 is when coffee starts.

Question 2
Prompt: Where will the herbs be planted?
A: On the west bed
B: On the east bed
C: In the shed
D: Beside the coffee
Correct: B
Explanation: Tomatoes go on the west bed. Herbs go on the east bed.
